Montri authorized the ijji repository, public website and ChatGPT Project source to adopt LDS 0.9.5 on 30 September 2026. This successor changes the **parent binding** for new ijji work. It inherits the ijji product-specific rules, motif approvals and limits of v0.5.1 and, through that release, v0.5.0. The predecessor files remain unchanged and continue to describe historical work based on LDS 0.9.1.

## 2. What carries forward

- Keep ijji's owner-first information order, calm and empathetic Thai/English voice, evidence labels, actionable next steps, privacy and product-truth limits. Locale Insight is a contextual prior only; it is not official population, eligibility, risk determination, statutory boundary or proof of behaviour. Aggregate only after a `locale_id` to service-boundary crosswalk.
- Keep exact, separately approved ijji identity assets and their recorded surfaces, roles and rights. The selected four-beat motifs remain ijji-only decorative orientation under v0.5.1. Their exact [registry and assets](../assets/motifs/ijji-four-beat-selected-3-r3/family-record.json) are retained in this repository with historical approval and source provenance. The registry's 0.9.1 binding is historical asset-pack provenance; this successor governs its current 0.9.5 compatibility without changing approved bytes or scope. They never become a logo, evidence mark, progress cue, score, map or business claim. Its fixed-surface static variants and external-image accessibility transport retain their v0.5.1 gates. Transparent and canvas variants remain unavailable for live use without a successor approval.
- The v0.5.1 four-beat motif motion decision stays `motionEnabled: false`. The separately approved, finite `ijji.logo-sting.r3` website identity is an artifact-local overlay with its own lifecycle, controls and fallback, not permission to animate the four-beat family.
- Preserve meaningful chart and table borders, visible focus outlines and responsive Thai typography. Selected navigation, tabs, cards and callouts use restrained fill and type weight: **no decorative bracket-shaped highlight or colored left-edge rail**.

## 3. Parent 0.9.5 application

The parent preserves the approved 0.9.1 brand voice, foundations, official fonts, core logo roles, semantic states and seven atmosphere recipes. Use the exact `color-srgb-08` package values for the revised dark categorical colors and analytical families.
